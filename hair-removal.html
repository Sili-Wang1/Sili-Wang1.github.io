Product concept · 2022

Hair Removal Device

A personal-care device concept informed by research into the European and North American markets. Through user, market, and competitor studies, I explored form, grip, and the interaction between the device and its user. The design translates these findings into a compact product with a restrained visual language and an emphasis on everyday handling.
