About me

Designing with care.
Making through curiosity.

I’m Sili Wang, a master’s student in Intelligent Manufacturing and Robotics at the School of Design, Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech), in Shenzhen, China. My research interests include social robotics, multisensory interaction, and mental health and well-being. My current research explores a robotic companion for desk-based micro-breaks.

I received my B.Eng. in Industrial Design from South China Agricultural University in 2024, graduating first in a cohort of 68 students. My undergraduate thesis explored haptic cycling navigation, combining product design, tactile feedback, and physical prototyping.

I am always open to new connections and potential collaborations. Feel free to drop me an email!

How I work

I combine research-through-design, physical prototyping, and user studies. My work spans embedded systems and interactive audio, alongside subjective and physiological evaluation to understand people’s experiences.

Beyond research, I enjoy hiking, traveling, and reading.
